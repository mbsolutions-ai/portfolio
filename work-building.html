Building & Markets

I have never had a conventional job. Since 2026 I have co-founded a software company, built and maintained platforms for two paying clients, and traded futures with my own money. This is where my communication training meets numbers: pricing, margins, risk, and explaining all of it to people who do not care about the technology.

Co-Founder · June 2026 – present · with two partners

plumline.ai

plumline is a software and web company I co-founded with two partners to serve contractors, starting with landscapers. The core product tells a contractor what one hour of a crew actually costs, truck by truck and service by service, instead of one blended guess. A quoting module checks every bid against that modeled cost before it goes out, and an offer-testing tool returns a live LTV:CAC keep-or-kill verdict on proposed marketing spend. Alongside the software, plumline builds premium websites for landscaping businesses that need to look as professional as their work.

On the technical side I developed the LLM API integrations and automated workflows. On the business side my job is the one my degree trained me for: explaining what the product does to business owners who do not care about the tech, and listening closely enough to learn what they would actually pay for.

My role
Co-founder. LLM integrations and workflow automation; customer-facing explanation and demos; shared responsibility for product direction and launch.
Skills demonstrated
Product development from idea to built software; unit economics (cost basis, margin, LTV:CAC); AI integration; and translating technical capability into plain language that sells.

KT Recruiting platform

KT Recruiting ranks kickers and punters for college programs. I built and maintain the platform, which carries 1,291 ranked athletes across 49 states and 14 countries, including a composite rating system that combines charted kicks, verified measurables, and film evaluation into one score. I have been retained on a monthly basis since launch.

This is the project where my two worlds overlap most. I know what a coach needs to see in a kicker's profile because I have been the kicker in the profile. That knowledge shaped what the rating system measures and how the pages are organized.

My role
Sole developer, from build through launch and ongoing maintenance. The engagement came through my professional network in the kicking community.
Skills demonstrated

Futures trading, and where it points next

Since February 2026 I have traded Nasdaq-100 (NQ) futures in my own account on TradingView. The rules matter more than any single trade: a defined intraday process, risk limits set before I enter, and broker statements reviewed to see what is and is not working. Trading has taught me to make decisions when the outcome is uncertain and to accept a loss without changing the plan, which is the same discipline kicking demands.

That experience is why I applied for Cargill's Commodity Trading Internship for summer 2027. The cover letter, included here as a writing sample, makes the case in plain language: eight months of trading my own money, a co-founded company built around pricing and margins, and a communications degree that trains the persuasion and negotiation side of merchandising.
